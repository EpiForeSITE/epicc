    Measles Outbreak Cost Estimation¶

    Estimates hospitalization, tracing, and productivity costs for measles outbreaks across three outbreak-size scenarios.

    Authors: Edward Wang, Olivia Banks, George G. Vega Yon, Richard Nelson

    Model id: measles. Open it in the app with ?model=measles; see Sharing links.

    About this model¶

    Overview¶

    This calculator estimates the economic burden of measles outbreaks by modeling three key cost components: direct medical costs (hospitalization), indirect costs (lost productivity from quarantine), and public health response costs (contact tracing).

    Scenarios¶

    The model evaluates three outbreak scenarios based on historical U.S. measles outbreaks:

    • 22 Cases: Small outbreak (typical in high-vaccination areas)
    • 100 Cases: Medium outbreak
    • 803 Cases: Large outbreak (similar to 2019 U.S. outbreak)

    Key Assumptions¶

    • All costs are in USD
    • Hospitalization rate and costs based on CDC surveillance data
    • Contact tracing effort scales linearly with case count
    • Lost productivity only counts quarantined unvaccinated contacts
    • Wage figures represent average hourly earnings

    Parameters¶

    Medical Costs¶

    Parameter Default Unit Range or choices Sources
    Cost of measles hospitalization
    cost_measles_hospitalization
    Average direct medical cost per hospitalized measles case, including hospital stay, treatments, and complications.    		 31,168 USD 0 to 500,000 Ortega-Sanchez et al. (2014). Economics of an outbreak: Cost of measles. Vaccine, 32(34).
    Proportion of cases hospitalized
    proportion_hospitalized
    Fraction of confirmed measles cases requiring hospital admission.    		 0.2 proportion 0 to 1 CDC Measles surveillance data (2018-2019).

    Lost Productivity¶

    Parameter Default Unit Range or choices Sources
    Proportion of quarantine days that would be a missed day of work
    proportion_missed_workdays
    Fraction of quarantine period that falls on workdays (accounting for weekends and non-working contacts).    		 0.5 proportion 0 to 1
    Hourly wage for worker
    hourly_wage_worker
    Average hourly wage for calculating lost productivity during quarantine.    		 29.36 USD/hour 0 to 500 U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (2024).
    Length of quarantine (days)
    quarantine_days
    Number of days unvaccinated contacts must quarantine to prevent disease spread.    		 21 days 0 to 30 CDC Measles quarantine guidelines (21 days from last exposure).
    Vaccination rate in community
    vaccination_rate
    Proportion of population with measles immunity (through vaccination or prior infection). Only unvaccinated contacts require quarantine.    		 0.8 proportion 0 to 1 CDC National Immunization Survey (2023).

    Contact Tracing¶

    Parameter Default Unit Range or choices Sources
    Number of contacts per case
    contacts_per_case
    Average number of close contacts identified per confirmed measles case.    		 141.5 contacts 0 to 500 Parker et al. (2006). Implications of a 2005 measles outbreak in Indiana for sustained elimination. NEJM.
    Hours of contact tracing per contact
    hours_contact_tracing_per_contact
    Staff time required to identify, notify, and monitor each contact.    		 0.832 hours 0 to 40 CDC Contact Tracing Guidelines for Measles.
    Hourly wage for contract tracer
    hourly_wage_contract_tracer
    Hourly wage for public health contact tracers.    		 40 USD/hour 0 to 200 Bureau of Labor Statistics median for epidemiologists/health educators (2024).

    Equations¶

    Evaluated in order; each can use the parameters and any equation above it.

    Hospitalization cost (USD) eq_hosp

    n_cases * proportion_hospitalized * cost_measles_hospitalization

    Lost productivity (USD) eq_lost_prod

    n_cases * contacts_per_case * (1 - vaccination_rate) * quarantine_days * proportion_missed_workdays * hourly_wage_worker

    Contact tracing cost (USD) eq_tracing

    n_cases * contacts_per_case * hours_contact_tracing_per_contact * hourly_wage_contract_tracer

    Total cost (USD) eq_total

    eq_hosp + eq_lost_prod + eq_tracing
