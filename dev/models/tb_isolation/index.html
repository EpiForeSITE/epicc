    TB Isolation Cost Estimation¶

    Estimates hospitalization, tracing, and productivity costs for TB isolation scenarios, comparing 14-day vs. 5-day isolation protocols.

    Authors: Edward Wang, Olivia Banks

    Model id: tb_isolation. Open it in the app with ?model=tb_isolation; see Sharing links.

    About this model¶

    Overview¶

    This calculator compares the economic impact of two tuberculosis isolation protocols: 14-day isolation (standard) versus 5-day isolation (shortened). The model accounts for direct isolation costs, lost productivity, and the long-term costs of secondary TB infections.

    $$ \text{LaTeX test.} $$

    Scenarios¶

    • 14-day Isolation: Standard isolation protocol for infectious TB patients
    • 5-day Isolation: Shortened isolation protocol (increases secondary transmission risk)

    Key Model Components¶

    1. Secondary Infection Estimation: Models latent and active TB cases resulting from reduced isolation time
    2. Direct Costs: Daily costs vary by isolation setting (hospital, motel, or home)
    3. Productivity Loss: Lost wages during isolation period (index case only)
    4. Secondary Infection Costs: Long-term costs of treating latent and active TB in contacts, with appropriate discounting over remaining years of life

    Economic Impact Summary¶

    The trade-off between isolation duration and transmission risk creates complex economic dynamics:

    • Short-term savings: Reduced isolation costs and productivity losses
    • Long-term costs: Higher burden from secondary infections
    • Net effect: Often favors longer isolation from a societal cost perspective

    Assumptions¶

    • All costs are in USD
    • Shortened isolation increases infectiousness by a multiplier
    • Latent TB progression to active disease occurs in two phases: first 2 years and remainder of lifetime
    • Future costs are discounted at the specified discount rate
    • Productivity loss assumes 8-hour workdays

    Equations¶

    Evaluated in order; each can use the parameters and any equation above it.

    Baseline latent TB infections (cases) eq_latent_baseline

    contacts_per_case * prob_latent_if_14_day

    Latent TB infections (cases) eq_latent

    eq_latent_baseline * latent_multiplier

    Active TB disease (cases) eq_active

    eq_latent * prob_latent_to_active_2yr + eq_latent * (1 - prob_latent_to_active_2yr) * prob_latent_to_active_lifetime

    Daily isolation cost (USD/day) eq_daily_cost

    (direct_medical_cost_day if (isolation_type == "HOSP_ISO" and direct_medical_cost_day > 0) else (isolation_cost if isolation_type == "HOSP_ISO" else (motel_room_cost + hourly_wage_nurse * nurse_checkin_hours if isolation_type == "MOTEL_ISO" else hourly_wage_nurse * nurse_checkin_hours)))

    Direct cost of isolation (USD) eq_direct

    eq_daily_cost * isolation_days

    Lost productivity (USD) eq_productivity

    isolation_days * workday_ratio * hourly_wage_worker * 8

    Discounted 2-year progression probability (dimensionless) eq_discounted_2yr

    (prob_latent_to_active_2yr / 2) / ((1 + discount_rate) ** 1) + (prob_latent_to_active_2yr / 2) / ((1 + discount_rate) ** 2)

    Cost per latent case (with discounting) (USD) eq_secondary_cost_per_latent

    cost_latent + cost_active * (eq_discounted_2yr + sum((prob_latent_to_active_lifetime / remaining_years_of_life) / ((1 + discount_rate) ** y) for y in range(3, remaining_years_of_life + 1)))

    Cost of secondary infections (USD) eq_secondary

    eq_latent * eq_secondary_cost_per_latent

    Total cost (USD) eq_total

    eq_direct + eq_productivity + eq_secondary
