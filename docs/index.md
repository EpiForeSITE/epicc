# EPICC: Epidemiological Cost Calculator

EPICC estimates what an outbreak costs, and what the public health response to it
costs. It runs entirely in your browser: pick a model, adjust its parameters and
scenarios, and read or export the report.

[Open the app](https://epiforesite.github.io/epicc/){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

The app is also deployed on Streamlit Community Cloud at
<https://epiworldpythonapp.streamlit.app/>.
